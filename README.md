## 👋 About me

<div align="justify">

Software Engineer (Universidad de Oviedo) and MSc in Artificial Intelligence (Universidad Politécnica de Madrid), based in Langreo, Asturias. I currently teach at the Universidad de Oviedo as a substitute professor and create food content as [@omarxteixeira](https://instagram.com/omarxteixeira).

My throughline has always been the same: taking artificial intelligence from research into the real world. During my internships and my Bachelor's/Master's thesis, I researched sleep quality in arthritis patients using wearable devices and machine learning / deep learning models, exploring transfer learning strategies to classify sleep stages — work that eventually led to a co-authored conference paper. Later, at ITCL Centro Tecnológico, I designed an audio source separation system based on Spiking Neural Networks, built with a modular architecture aimed at scalability and long-term maintainability.

Outside the lab I equally enjoy full-stack development: databases, backend, frontend and deployment, with the same attention to quality I bring to an ML model. And this year I lived a completely different adventure: I was a **contestant on MasterChef Spain, season 14** — an experience that taught me as much about teamwork under pressure as any development sprint.

<p align="center"><b>My personal project</b></p>

<div align="justify">

**[RatioX.net](https://ratiox.net)** is a recipes and cooking-content website that I design, build, and maintain solo, with connected recipes to make the most of ingredients, time, and money. It was born out of my time on **MasterChef Spain, season 14**, and has a presence on Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, and X as [@omarxteixeira](https://instagram.com/omarxteixeira).

It's more than a blog: it's a small product with its own modular JavaScript frontend (no frameworks), a search bar with filters by ingredients, course, dish, time, difficulty, diet, content type, and series, an interactive **graph of connected recipes** ("Map"), a shopping list, favorites, and a "Kitchen" section. Deployed as a static site on **Cloudflare Pages**, with Google Analytics behind a custom-built cookie banner.

</div>

`HTML5` · `CSS3` · `JavaScript` · `Cloudflare Pages` · `PWA`
